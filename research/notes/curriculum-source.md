# Conclusion

The applicable national baseline is the MEXT **平成29年告示 (2017 notification) elementary and junior-high Courses of Study**. MEXT's current Course-of-Study hub links the official text and subject explanations for both school levels. MEXT states that the elementary standard became fully effective in FY2020 and the junior-high standard in FY2021. The current MEXT education-data page still identifies the elementary code table as 82V12 (平成29年告示, v1.2; published 2022-03-14) and junior-high as 83V11 (平成29年告示, v1.1; published 2021-03-18), with no newer replacement listed. Sources: hub and implementation statement [MEXT hub](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/shotou/new-cs/1384661.htm?nt=1); implementation dates [MEXT/スポーツ庁 page](https://www.mext.go.jp/sports/b_menu/sports/mcatetop04/list/1398875.htm#学習指導要領); code-table status [MEXT education data standard](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/other/data_00001.htm#教育データ標準).

The tables below are a product-ready, non-sequential curriculum tree. Japanese headings in quotation marks are the official content headings in MEXT's explanatory overview; short English descriptions are faithful compressions of the listed bullets. A label such as “Fractions” is an **editorial grouping** when MEXT lists the material under a longer heading (for example, 「分数の意味と表し方」); it is not a claim that MEXT prescribes textbook unit order or a canonical lesson title.

# Grade-by-grade findings

## Elementary school (算数)

The official domains are A 数と計算 (number and calculation), B 図形 (geometry), C 測定 (measurement; later 変化と関係), and D データの活用 (data use). “〔数学的活動〕” is a cross-domain way of learning, not an additional drill unit.

| Grade | Official heading / product label (editorial labels marked) | Concise content covered |
|---|---|---|
| 小1 | A 「数の構成と表し方」 | Compare and count objects; ordinal/cardinal numbers; order, size and number line; represent 2-digit and simple 3-digit numbers; tens as a unit; grouping and equal sharing. |
| 小1 | A 「加法，減法」 | Meanings and situations for addition/subtraction; equations; addition facts and inverse subtraction; simple 2-digit cases. |
| 小1 | B 「図形についての理解の基礎」 | Notice shape features; compose/decompose shapes; direction and position. |
| 小1 | C 「量と測定についての理解の基礎」 | Direct/indirect comparison of quantities; compare using informal units. |
| 小1 | C 「時刻の読み方」 | Read clock times. |
| 小1 | D 「絵や図を用いた数量の表現」 | Represent quantities with pictures and diagrams. |
| 小2 | A 「数の構成と表し方」 | Count/group/classify; decimal place-value system; relative size; view a number as a product; classify/organize by number; simple fractions such as 1/2 and 1/3. |
| 小2 | A 「加法，減法」 | 2-digit addition/subtraction; simple 3-digit cases; properties of addition/subtraction; inverse relationship. |
| 小2 | A 「乗法」 | Situations and meaning; multiplication equations; simple properties; multiplication tables; simple 2-digit × 1-digit cases. |
| 小2 | B 「三角形や四角形などの図形」 | Triangles/quadrilaterals; square, rectangle, right triangle; boxes made from square/rectangular faces. |
| 小2 | C 「長さ，かさの単位と測定」 | Units and measurement of length/capacity; estimation and choosing a suitable unit. |
| 小2 | C 「時間の単位」 | Units of time and their relationships. |
| 小2 | D 「簡単な表やグラフ」 | Read and make simple tables and graphs. |
| 小3 | A 「数の表し方」 | Ten-thousand; 10/100/1000 times; relative size. |
| 小3 | A 「加法，減法」 | 3- and 4-digit addition/subtraction algorithms and fluency. |
| 小3 | A 「乗法」 | 2-/3-digit × 1-/2-digit multiplication; reliable use; properties. |
| 小3 | A 「除法」 | Meaning and situations; equations; relation to multiplication/subtraction; 1-digit divisor/quotient and simple 2-digit quotient cases. |
| 小3 | A 「小数の意味と表し方」 | Meaning/representation; decimal addition and subtraction. |
| 小3 | A 「分数の意味と表し方」 | Meaning/representation; unit-fraction multiples; simple fraction addition/subtraction. |

| 小3 | A 「そろばん」 | Represent numbers and calculate with the soroban. |

| 小3 | C 「長さ，重さの単位と測定」 | Units/measurement of length and mass; choose suitable unit and instrument. |
| 小3 | C 「時刻と時間」 | Seconds; determine time and elapsed time. |
| 小3 | D 「表と棒グラフ」 | Classify/organize data in tables; features and use of bar graphs. |
| 小4 | A 「整数の表し方」 | Units of 100 million (億) and trillion (兆). |
| 小4 | A 「概数と四捨五入」 | When to use approximation; rounding; estimate results of the four operations. |

| 小5 | B 「立体図形の性質」 | Prisms and cylinders. |
| 小5 | B 「平面図形の面積」 | Calculate triangle, parallelogram, rhombus and trapezoid areas. |

## Junior-high school (数学)

MEXT organizes every grade under four official domains: A 数と式 (number and algebraic expressions), B 図形 (geometry), C 関数 (functions), D データの活用 (data use). The headings below are the official overview headings; descriptions are short compressions.

| Grade | Official heading / product label (editorial labels marked) | Concise content covered |
|---|---|---|
| 中1 | A 「正の数・負の数」 | Need/meaning of positive and negative numbers; four operations; represent situations with signed numbers; prime-number terminology and express a natural number as a product of primes. |
| 中1 | A 「文字を用いた式」 | Why/meaning of letters; multiplication/division notation; add/subtract linear expressions; express quantities with letters. |
| 中1 | A 「一元一次方程式」 | Need/meaning and solution of equations; solve one-variable linear equations (including proportional equations). |
| 中1 | B 「平面図形」 | Basic constructions; transformations; reason about construction methods. |
| 中1 | B 「空間図形」 | Relative positions of lines/planes; basic measurement; construct and represent solids on a plane. |
| 中1 | C 「比例，反比例」 | Function relationship; direct/inverse proportion; coordinates; tables, equations and graphs. |
| 中1 | D 「データの分布の傾向」 | Need/meaning of histograms and relative frequency; read distributions. |
| 中1 | D 「多数の観察や多数回の試行によって得られる確率」 | Need/meaning of empirical probability from many observations/trials; cumulative frequency terminology. |
| 中2 | A 「文字を用いた式の四則計算」 | Add/subtract simple polynomials; multiply/divide monomials; express/read and explain with formulas; purposeful algebraic transformation. |
| 中2 | A 「連立二元一次方程式」 | Meaning and solutions of two-variable linear systems; solve systems. |
| 中2 | B 「基本的な平面図形と平行線の性質」 | Parallel lines and angles; polygon angle properties; verify plane-figure properties. |
| 中2 | B 「図形の合同」 | Congruence and triangle congruence criteria; need/meaning/method of proof; counterexamples. |
| 中2 | C 「一次関数」 | Situations and linear functions; two-variable linear equations as functions; tables, formulas and graphs. |
| 中2 | D 「データの分布の比較」 | Need/meaning and construction of quartile range and box plots; compare distributions. |
| 中2 | D 「場合の数を基にして得られる確率」 | Need/meaning of probability; calculate probability from counts of cases. |
| 中3 | A 「平方根」 | Need/meaning and representation of square roots; calculate expressions containing roots; error, approximation, and a×10ⁿ notation. |
| 中3 | A 「式の展開と因数分解」 | Multiply/divide monomials and polynomials; expand and factor simple expressions. |
| 中3 | A 「二次方程式」 | Meaning and solutions; solve by factoring/completing the square; quadratic formula. |
| 中3 | B 「図形の相似」 | Similar figures and triangle criteria; similarity/area/volume ratios; parallel lines and segment ratios. |
| 中3 | B 「円周角と中心角」 | Relationship and proof between inscribed (circumference) and central angles. |
| 中3 | B 「三平方の定理」 | Pythagorean theorem and proof. |
| 中3 | C 「関数 y＝ax²」 | Situations and varied examples; tables, formulas and graphs of y=ax². |
| 中3 | D 「標本調査」 | Need/meaning of sampling surveys; draw and organize a sample. |

Cross-domain mathematical activity (〔数学的活動〕) is required throughout: mathematize daily/social or mathematical situations, represent/process and solve, reflect and generalize, and explain using mathematical representations. It is a learning process requirement, not a separate topical unit.

# Terminology and implementation notes

- “Unit” is an editorial product concept. The Courses of Study specify domains, grade content, and bullets—not a national textbook sequence, lesson count, or one fixed set of unit names. Keep the Japanese official heading in metadata and use the concise product label only as a UI grouping.
- The 2017 revision changed junior-high 「資料の活用」 to 「データの活用」 and redistributed some content: elementary Grade 6 receives representative values; junior-high Grade 1 receives empirical probability; Grade 2 adds quartile range/box plots; Grade 3 receives error/approximation and prime-factorization content moved from other grades. The official explanation's transition table is PDF pp. 14–16 (printed pp. 12–14).
- Elementary Grade 5 includes a programming experience linked to a B 図形 activity in the normative Course of Study's content-handling section. This is a teaching activity constraint, not a separate arithmetic drill topic.
- “円周率” is the official Japanese term in the Grade 5 geometry overview; in a user-facing English table, “pi/circumference constant” is explanatory gloss.

Confidence is high for scope, grade placement, headings, and listed concepts because they are taken from MEXT's current official text/explanation. The exact page/line numbering can differ between browser-extracted PDF text and printed pagination; use the PDF's printed chapter/page anchors above.
